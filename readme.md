The goal of this project was to overlay Spring wildfires and that flooding that hurricane Helene caused to see if there was a connection between the two. The hurricane was represented by rainfall data from the National Weather Service, and accounted for the average rainfall of each county. That provided the background choropleth map. The interactive choropleth lab template from class was used to guide this section. Then the act of finding data for the wildfires turned out to be a challenge, as people seem most interested in current events rather than what has very recently happened. Luckily the National Forest Service has a catalog of press releases which allowed the major fires to be found. This did skew the data to focus on National Forest fires, but because of the size of the fires that doesn't seem to change the outcome too much. Adding the points to the map was not difficult, but making the map prettier felt like trying to dig a trench with your front teeth. I just could not draw from my experience or the internet in a way that fit what I had envisioned, and when I would try to get creative the map would disappear. I am turning it in a very basic form, and we will see if changing it into a bootstrap form will happen.
